The Problem.

The accounting firm was bogged down by constant inbound calls, repetitive client inquiries, and massive data triage bottlenecks. Highly paid operational staff were forced into acting as receptionists, aggressively cutting into billable hours and entirely paralyzing the firm's ability to scale during tax seasons.

Platform Architecture.

We deployed a dual-engine architecture. A localized AI Front Desk flawlessly handles all phonetic inbound traffic, booking appointments, and resolving tier-1 client queries autonomously round-the-clock. Simultaneously, an internal AI Copilot agent plugs directly into their datastore, accelerating document extraction and supercharging raw staff execution bandwidth.
