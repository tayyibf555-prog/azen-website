The Problem.

The firm's stock sourcing operations were bogged down by decentralized spreadsheets and constant manual communication loops with suppliers. As client demands scaled, their procurement desk was paralyzed by data entry overhead, severely losing critical lead time in acquiring high-demand domestic and global stock.

Platform Architecture.

We engineered a proprietary sourcing CRM tailored perfectly to their operational needs. The automated system actively aggregates supplier data streams, validates stock availability natively, and consolidates the entire procurement pipeline into a single, highly operative execution dashboard. dramatically increasing their overall supply chain velocity.
